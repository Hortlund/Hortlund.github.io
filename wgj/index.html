Is We Go Jim free?

Yes. We Go Jim is free to download from the App Store.

Can I use it offline?

Yes. Core workouts, templates, history, exercises, active workout drafts, and profile features are designed to remain usable on your device.

What can I track?

Sets, reps, weight, cardio blocks, rest timers, notes, personal records, training volume, workout history, templates, goals, and progress trends.
